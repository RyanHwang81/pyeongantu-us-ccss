# CCSS backtest snapshot (2026-09-14 build)

Expanding-window CCSS from official series. Thresholds 60/75 were not fitted to 2008 or 2020.

## Coverage limit

NY Fed HHDC national tables start 2003 Q1. After YoY transforms and a 20-observation minimum, CCSS first prints in **2008 Q3**. 2000–2002 cannot be scored with these series and is not invented.

## Read-through

- **GFC:** By the time CCSS can be computed, the score is already crisis-level. The monitor does not claim a 2006–07 early warning from these public NY Fed tables.
- **2020:** Forbearance crushed reported delinquency and charge-offs. CCSS fell. This is a data feature, not a reason to retune weights.
- **2022–23:** Tightening cycle pushed CCSS into the 60s (credit stress) without a 75 recession-signal print. Matches a consumer-stress scare that did not become a credit crisis.
- **Now (2026 Q2):** 55.3 WATCH, momentum Early Warning, recession signal WATCH (serious-delinquency flow up; SLOOS tightening + revolving growth below its median). Charge-offs are not yet in a two-quarter rising trend.

Quarters with CCSS ≥ 75: 6. Of those, 4 were followed by NBER recession within 12 months; 2 were not. Do not treat 75 as a hard recession switch.
